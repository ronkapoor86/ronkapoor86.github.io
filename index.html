I started as a developer at Jefferies, building data platforms and APIs for trading desks. At CelerData, I combined hands-on engineering contributions to StarRocks with developer relations, technical education and open-source community building. At Conduktor, I brought together product marketing, GTM and developer advocacy, repositioning the company, leading product launches and building its technical content and demo engine.

Across these roles, I've worked at the intersection of engineering, product, marketing and business, understanding both the people building with technology and the commercial realities of getting infrastructure products adopted.
